# Week 5 — Patterns: Spot & Extend
**Primary 1 · Term 1 · Single Class: 60–70 minutes**

---

## Overview

Students discover repeating patterns in sound, movement, and objects. They learn to name the core — the repeating unit — and extend a pattern correctly. This is the first of two pattern weeks: this week is about recognition, next week is about creation.

---

## Purpose

To introduce pattern recognition as a foundational computational thinking skill. Students who can spot a core unit in a repeating sequence are beginning to think abstractly — identifying the rule, not just the individual items.

---

## Objectives

- Identify a repeating pattern in sound, movement, and objects
- Name the core (the repeating unit) in a pattern
- Extend a pattern correctly by at least 3 more repetitions
- Create a simple AB or ABC pattern using claps, movements, or objects

---

## Preparation

- Gather 15–20 bottle caps or counters in at least 2 colours per pair — OR prepare coloured paper circles as an alternative
- If using video: download or queue up the video
- If no screen: rehearse the movement pattern game (stand-sit-jump sequence)
- Estimated prep time: 15 minutes

## Key Vocabulary

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Pattern** | Something that repeats in a predictable way |
| **Core** | The repeating unit inside a pattern |
| **AB pattern** | Two-item repeating unit: red-blue, red-blue… |
| **ABC pattern** | Three-item repeating unit: clap-snap-stamp… |
| **Extend** | Continue a pattern by adding more repetitions |

---

## Teaching Guide

### 1. Bridge from Week 4 *(5 min)*

Ask 3–4 volunteers to share their Page 12 sequences from home. Ask if anyone circled a repeating section in their sequence. Hold up any example and say: *"When something repeats, we call that a pattern — and that is what we are learning today."*

---

### 2. Warm-Up — Clapping Rhythm Patterns *(10 min)*

Teacher claps a rhythm: clap-clap-pause, clap-clap-pause. Class joins in. Then ask: *"What is repeating?"* Name the core together.

Work through three levels:
- AB: clap-stamp, clap-stamp…
- AAB: clap-clap-stamp, clap-clap-stamp…
- ABC: clap-snap-stamp, clap-snap-stamp…

After each round, ask: *"What is the core?"* Write **PATTERN** and **CORE** on the board.

---

### 3. Activity — Bottle Cap Strings *(10 min)*

Pairs build a repeating pattern string using bottle caps in different colours, then swap with another pair who must extend it by 4 more caps.

After swapping: *"How did you know what came next?"*

No materials? Students draw coloured shapes on paper — same activity, no caps needed.

---

### 4. Video — "Pattern Dance!" *(4 min)*

Play after the bottle cap activity. Students join in with the movement patterns on screen.

> **No screen?** Teacher leads the movement game live: stand-sit-jump-stand-sit-jump. Students join in and predict what comes next before the teacher does it.

---

### 5. Activity — Body Movement Patterns *(10 min)*

Teacher leads increasingly complex movement patterns. Students do the action and say it aloud together.

Then students take turns leading: one student performs 4 repetitions of a secret pattern; the class names the core and predicts what comes 5th.

---

### 6. Activity Book — Pages 13 & 14 *(15 min)*

Page 13: students circle the core in each of 4 pattern rows and draw what comes next. Row 4 has a broken pattern — students must find what is wrong and fix it.

Page 14: students extend 4 partial patterns by drawing the next 3 items.

Page 15 goes home: students find 3 real-world patterns at home or on the way to school, draw each one, and circle the core.

---

### 7. Share & Close *(10 min)*

Ask 3 volunteers to share a pattern from Page 14. Class extends each one together.

**Bridge:** *"Computers love patterns — they use them to do the same thing over and over without being told each time. Next week you will create your own."*
